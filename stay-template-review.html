What this is

One template for every STAY property. Drop in a property’s content fields and the page is done — no per-property layout work. The section order, surface rhythm, and booking placement are fixed; only the content changes. The reference bar is 1 Hotels and Ace: photography carries the page, copy is short, and the guest can book from anywhere on it.

Decisions already made

  • Booking & rates run on StayNTouch, our current RMS. The earlier Mews direction is dead and its code is removed. Until the StayNTouch feed is built, the page shows no prices — rate badges stay hidden rather than showing a number we can’t back up.
  • All copy facts come from the live property page. Suite mix and specs (studio / one / two bedroom — 517 / 673 / 1,053 sq ft), the 19-amenity list, and venue naming (PHX Arena) match sentral.com/phoenix/sol-modern/stay exactly.
  • SEO is built in: every section has a stable anchor link for campaigns and search (e.g. /stay/sol-modern#suites, #amenities, #neighborhood), and the page carries schema.org Hotel structured data for rich search results.

What we want your input on

  • Overall look — does this feel like Sentral, and does it sell STAY better than the current pages?
  • Section order — anything missing, anything you’d cut?
  • Suite cards — is spec-only (sleeps / sq ft / bed) enough, or do suites need their own pages?
  • Guest reviews — the current site shows Google reviews; this template has no social proof yet. Should it?
  • URL shape — demo lives at /stay/sol-modern; today’s pages are /phoenix/sol-modern/stay.

Photography is placeholder. The template is only as good as its assets — per property it needs a hero, two story images, four to six amenity tiles, a photo and floor plan per suite type, and a gallery set. Judge the layout, not the photos.
