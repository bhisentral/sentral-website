These Reservation Policies apply to STAY bookings at Sentral properties made through sentral.com or our reservations team. Property-specific terms — parking rates, pet fees, and local requirements — are confirmed at booking and in the Good to Know section of each property page.

Draft for review: items marked [FIELD] require confirmation from operations before this page publishes. Nothing here supersedes the terms presented at booking.

01

Check-In & Check-Out

Check-in begins at 4:00 PM local time with keyless self check-in; your entry code arrives by text and email the morning of arrival. Check-out is by 11:00 AM. Late check-out until 2:00 PM is subject to availability and may carry a fee. [FIELD — confirm with operations]

A government-issued photo ID matching the reservation name is required for every stay.

02

Payment & Deposits

A valid credit or debit card is required to reserve. Flexible rates are charged at check-in; pre-paid rates (including Advance Purchase offers) are charged in full at booking and are non-refundable.

An incidental hold may be placed at check-in and released after departure, subject to your bank’s processing times. [FIELD — confirm with operations]

03

Cancellation

Most flexible rates cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before arrival; inside 48 hours, one night’s room and tax is charged. Rates marked non-refundable cannot be cancelled or refunded.

Offer and promotional rates carry the cancellation terms shown at booking. Blackout dates and terms apply to promotional rates.

04

Rates, Taxes & Fees

The rate you see is the rate you pay, plus applicable state and local taxes. Sentral charges no resort fee and no hidden charges.

Parking, where offered, is charged per night at the rate posted for the property. [FIELD — confirm with operations]

05

Occupancy & Minimum Age

Guests must be at least 18 years of age to reserve and check in. [FIELD — confirm with operations]

Maximum occupancy per suite is stated on each suite type and includes children. Events and parties are not permitted in suites.

06

Pets

Pets are welcome at Sentral. Pet fees, weight limits, and breed policies vary by property and are confirmed at booking. [FIELD — confirm with operations]

Service animals are always welcome and never carry a fee.

07

Smoking

All Sentral suites and interior common areas are smoke-free. A deep-cleaning fee applies to any violation. [FIELD — confirm with operations]

08

Group Reservations

Booking four or more suites? Contact our group sales team for special pricing, room blocks, and a single contract — Group Travel & Room Blocks, or email Sales@Sentral.com.

09

Extended Stays (30+ Nights)

Stays of 30 nights or more book at extended-stay rates and may be governed by a short-term occupancy agreement in some jurisdictions. [FIELD — confirm with operations]

Some properties host extended stays exclusively; minimum-stay requirements are shown at booking (31 nights in Los Angeles).

10

Property Care & Damages

Guests are responsible for damage to the suite or its furnishings beyond normal wear. Repair or replacement costs may be charged to the card on file. [FIELD — confirm with operations]

11

Accessibility

Accessible suites and features vary by property and may be requested at booking. Contact the property directly to confirm specific accommodations before arrival.

12

Contact

Questions about a reservation? Reach the property team via the number on your confirmation, or email Contact@sentral.com.
