What this is

A working prototype of the full STAY booking journey — search, room selection, checkout, confirmation — plus the city selector for multi-property markets. Everything is clickable and every hand-off carries real state (property, dates, rooms, rate plan), so you can walk a booking start to finish exactly the way a guest would. It wears the same chrome and design language as the rest of the site.

Decisions already made

  • All live data comes from StayNTouch via SentralOS (our current RMS — Mews is dead). Pages 2, 4, and 6 are marked with every pull point; the pull method is being confirmed with Nathan.
  • Payment stays on Shift4, embedded unchanged. The prototype deliberately renders no card fields — the dashed panel on checkout marks exactly where the embed drops in.
  • Search & Availability is the P0 page. Today's funnel converts 5% at this step — the largest single drop-off in the journey — so it gets the most scrutiny.
  • Multi-room is native to search: quantity steppers per suite type with a running total, rather than a separate group flow.
  • Rate plans mirror the live offers — Direct, Advance Purchase (−20%), Seasonal Sale — and the property page's Offers cards carry their plan into the funnel.

What we want your input on

  • The search results page — does the suite-card + stepper + summary-rail layout earn its P0 status? What would make you book?
  • Room detail — is the three-plan rate list clear? Is a separate room page even needed, or should plans live on search results?
  • Checkout — anything missing before the payment step? (Loyalty/SentralPlus capture? Arrival time?)
  • City selector — right way to handle Austin, Charlotte, LA, and Miami?
  • Confirmation — what should it push next? (App download, SentralPlus enrollment, upsells?)

Every rate, availability flag, and tax line is sample data, and every funnel page carries a ribbon saying so. Photography is placeholder. Judge the flow, the layout, and the copy structure — not the numbers or the photos.
