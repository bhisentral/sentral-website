02

Cancellation & Payment Policy

Completion of guest registration portal required for all guests. Credit card is collected at the time of booking.

For reservations of 29 consecutive nights or less
Full room rate, plus taxes and fees will be charged to the credit card on file 48 hours prior to arrival. Reservations are refundable up to 48 hours prior to arrival. One night room and tax penalty applies for cancellations within 48 hours of stay.

For advance purchase reservations
Full room rate, plus taxes and fees will be charged to the credit card on file upon booking. This is a pre-paid and non-refundable booking. No cancellations, changes, or refunds are permitted.

For reservations of 30 consecutive nights or longer
Credit card payment for 30% of stay is collected at booking. The remaining balance of stay is collected 7 days prior to arrival. If reservation confirmed within a week of arrival, full payment is taken upon booking. Reservations are fully refundable provided the cancellation occurs 28 days or more before check in. For cancellations less than 28 days before arrival, the first 30 days of the stay are non refundable. A signed lease agreement is required.
